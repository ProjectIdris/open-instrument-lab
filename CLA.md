## 1. What a "Contribution" is

Any code, text, data, picture or other work that you send to the project to be included in it (a pull request, a patch, a file in an issue), and that you have not marked clearly as "Not a Contribution". A Contribution does not include anything you send that is not meant to be included, such as a question.

You keep the copyright in your Contribution. You may use it yourself in any way, including in other projects. The Project Owner may keep a record of who contributed what, and your name stays in the history of the repository.

## 4. Ending

The licences in section 2 are permanent, and the Project Owner's rights pass to whoever the Project Owner passes the project to. **Once you have sent a Contribution, you cannot withdraw it or the licence to it**, whether or not it was merged, released or later removed. You can stop agreeing for **future** Contributions at any time by saying so in an issue; that changes nothing for what you have already sent. The only way a licence in section 2 ends is the patent clause in 2.2, and only for the patent case it describes.

## 5. How to agree

Open your pull request with the project's template. It has one line you must keep, which says that you have read this agreement and that you agree to it for that pull request and for every Contribution you send afterwards until you say otherwise in an issue (section 4). **The line is your signature.** There is no form to sign, no account to create and no service to use.

If you are sending a Contribution for a company, or the company has rights in it, the company must agree too: ask in an issue first, and the Project Owner will agree a version for the company.
